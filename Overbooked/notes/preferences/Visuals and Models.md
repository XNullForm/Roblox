# Visuals and Models

Back to [[My Preferences]]

**Likes**
- **Stunning graphics** and realistic lighting: a real sky, sun and shadows, haze, colour grading, realistic terrain materials.
- Models in a **"slightly more realistic"** style: smooth, textured, soft colour blends. Not fully photoreal.
- **Lots of asset variety**: different versions of each tree, rock and bush.
- Nature worlds full of trees, grass, flowers and bushes, with each area looking different.

**Dislikes**
- Low-poly, faceted models.
- Assets floating off the ground.
- Empty-looking patches of map.
- AI-generated looking textures (the dirt path texture had to go back to normal).
