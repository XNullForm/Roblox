# VFX Preferences

Back to [[My Preferences]]

**Likes**
- Particles everywhere they make sense: dust, leaves, sparks, splashes, region atmosphere.
- **Natural** effects: a dash made of wind, gusts and torn-up ground.
- Speed effects that build with speed (slipstream ribbons, speed lines, vignette).

**Dislikes**
- **Neon / glowing** effects on moves.
- "Air slashes": wind streaks rushing past the body at high speed.
- Other players' effects not showing or out of sync.
