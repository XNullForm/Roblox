# SFX Preferences

Back to [[My Preferences]]

**Likes**
- Layered, punchy sounds timed exactly on the action.
- Airy, natural wind sounds for fast moves.
- Ambience per area (surf, wind, birds, chimes).

**Dislikes**
- Sci-fi charge-ups and motor sounds.
- Footsteps that are too loud.
- Roblox's default character sounds.
- Other players' sounds out of sync with what you see.
