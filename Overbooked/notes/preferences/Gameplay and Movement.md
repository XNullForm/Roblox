# Gameplay and Movement

Back to [[My Preferences]]

**Likes**
- **Great movement**: momentum that matters, hills that change your speed, speed on flat ground too.
- Lots of moves and mechanics, and **"a lot more content"** to keep it fun.
- Progression: a skill tree with unlockable moves, NPC quests, achievements.
- Shift lock, and the head following the camera.
- Big open worlds with locations **very spread out**, that you have to run around to discover.
- ~~Being asked about the tutorial on every join~~ (changed 2026-10-20: no prompt; a GO TO TRAINING option in settings instead).
- Mechanics spread all over the world, with a reason to use them (parkour that's useful, rails and ziplines as fast routes) (2026-10-20).
- Plenty of enemies everywhere, with simple 1/2/3 skills (2026-10-20).
- No cutscenes in the way: after a tutorial, just put me in the world (2026-10-20).

**Dislikes**
- Exploits that make things too easy: spamming moves to max speed, tricking many times in one jump, holding a key to auto-trigger a move.
- Speed that builds too fast (about 4 s to top speed felt right in Straight Away).
- Slow, sluggish uphill movement.
- Slides you can steer too easily or ride backwards.
- Big speed boosts from air dashes.
