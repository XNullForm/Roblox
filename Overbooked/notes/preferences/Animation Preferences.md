# Animation Preferences

Back to [[My Preferences]]

> [!important] The quality bar is the Straight Away idle animations: "i really like all of the idle animations their quality is very good". Every new animation follows the [[Straight Away docs/Animation Style Guide|Animation Style Guide]] (12 rules and the process), in any game.

**Likes**
- Emotion and energy; idles that are fluid, bouncy, and **randomly do a stretch**.
- **Real limb rotation** (twist and roll), not just swinging.
- Runs that change clearly with speed: walk, then a real arm-pumping run, then a really-fast phase that kicks in at a moderate speed.

**Dislikes**
- Stiff, sine-wave looking animation.
- Arms flapping or crossing the chest, limbs out of sync, legs kicking out.
- A run so subtle it looks like the walk.
- Too much body tilt when turning.
